Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

Connect 4 vs AI (the "Site") is a free browser game available at davidbai20100918-glitch.github.io/first-folder. This policy explains what information the optional comment section collects and how it is used.

1. What we collect

You can play the game without signing in. Signing in is only needed to post a comment. If you choose to sign in with Google, we receive the following from your Google account:

  • your display name;
  • your email address;
  • the URL of your profile picture;
  • your Google account identifier (a stable, non-guessable ID).

When you post a comment, we also store the comment text and the time it was posted.

2. What we do not collect

  • We never receive or store your Google password.
  • We do not request access to Gmail, Contacts, Drive, or any other Google service.
  • We do not use advertising trackers or third-party analytics.
  • We do not sell or rent any data to anyone.

3. How the information is used

Your name and profile picture are displayed publicly next to the comments you post, so that other visitors can tell who wrote what. Your email address is stored to help moderate the comment section and to contact you about your comments if necessary. It is never displayed publicly.

4. Where the information is stored

Comments and account details are stored in a Cloudflare D1 database operated by Cloudflare, Inc. The Site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

When you sign in, a session token is saved in your browser's localStorage so that you stay signed in. It expires after seven days. Signing out, or clearing your browser storage, removes it.

5. Retention and deletion

Comments remain until they are deleted. You may request deletion of your comments, and of the account details we hold about you, at any time by emailing the address below. We will action such requests within a reasonable period.

6. Children

The Site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

7. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published on this page with a new "last updated" date.
